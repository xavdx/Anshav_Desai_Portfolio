Reaction test

Five red lights, then lights out. Tap the gantry (or press Space) to arm it, then react the moment the lights go out. Go early and it is a jump start. Do five and the panel beside it scores how consistent you were.

Times include your screen and input lag, so treat them as a fun number rather than a lab result.
